Before you post a problem

Send the log. Without it nobody can tell what went wrong.

Copy the crash report

When the game crashes, the launcher shows The game crashed with a short reason. Press Copy report. The launcher says when it is on the clipboard.

Use safe mode starts the next run with mods and the experimental engine options off. If the game works in safe mode, one of those caused it.

Or export the log

No crash window? In the launcher open More › Log, then Export… Do it right after the problem, before you start the game again.

The last session's log is also saved as logs\last_session.log in the Lantern Engine folder.

Post it in #issues

One thread per problem in the #issues forum on the Discord. Attach the log as a file and say where you were and what you pressed.

For anything visual, add a short video or a screenshot, and say whether it also happens with mods off.

The game crashed: copy the crash report

When the game crashes, the launcher shows The game crashed with a short reason. Press Copy report and paste it into your post. It has your version, GPU, driver, settings and the end of the log.

Use safe mode starts the next run with mods and the experimental engine options off. If the game works in safe mode, one of those caused it.

How do I copy or export my log?

In the launcher: More › Log, then Copy or Export… Do it right after the problem happens, before you start the game again.

The log of the last session is also saved as logs\last_session.log in the Lantern Engine folder.

What else to put in a report
  • The log or crash report (above).
  • What you were doing: where in the game, what you pressed, how long it ran.
  • For anything visual (flicker, missing or stretched textures): a short video or a screenshot.
  • Whether it also happens with mods off.

Post it in the #issues forum on the Discord, one thread per problem.

Why was my Discord message deleted, or why was I muted?

The Discord's bot deletes messages with these words and mutes the sender for 60 minutes, because they are how people ask for pirated game files.

  • Anywhere in a message, even inside another word: eboot eboot.bin
  • As a word on its own: dump download game files how to download files rom boot
  • Say "game folder" or "my copy of the game" instead.
  • "Download" counts too, even for the launcher: say "get it from the releases page".
  • Send logs as a file, not pasted lines: More › Log › Export…, or Copy report after a crash (Discord turns a long paste into a file). Log lines contain some of these words.

Getting started

Lantern Engine runs your own copy of Bloodborne on a Windows or Linux PC.

Is this legal?

Lantern Engine is free, open-source software (GPL). It contains no game files: no Bloodborne data, no PS4 system files, nothing from Sony or FromSoftware. It is not affiliated with either of them.

To play, you need your own legal copy of Bloodborne, dumped from your own PS4 with your own disc or digital purchase, plus the 1.09 update. Downloading the game from the internet instead of dumping your own copy is piracy, and this project does not support it: nobody here shares or links to game files. Don't ask for them in the Discord.

What do I need?
  • Your own legal copy of Bloodborne CUSA03173 with the 1.09 update, dumped and decrypted from your own PS4 (the folder with eboot.bin and sce_module). No game files come with Lantern Engine.
  • Windows 10 or 11, or Linux (Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, Arch, Fedora, SteamOS, Bazzite and similar).
  • A Vulkan graphics card with a current driver. Players run it on NVIDIA GTX 10 series and newer, and AMD RX 400 series and newer.
  • The game takes about 6 GB of memory (RAM plus page file), about 10 GB above 1080p. 16 GB of RAM is comfortable.
How do I install it and play?
  1. Get the latest package from Releases and unpack it somewhere you can write to (not Program Files, not a OneDrive folder).
  2. Open Lantern Engine.exe (on Linux: Lantern Engine).
  3. On the Play page, choose the game folder.
  4. Accept Settings for this PC when the launcher offers them. You can get them again later from Graphics › Best settings for this PC › Recommend…
  5. Press Play.
How do I update?

The launcher updates itself and the engine: Settings › Updates. Your settings, saves, mods and logs are kept. To get test builds early, see the Beta guide.

If the Play page shows Engine update available, press Update engine. Playing on an old engine means the crash fixes since then are missing.

On Linux the launcher updates itself too. Packages older than 0.9.37 beta 7 are updated by getting the new one from Releases.

Does it work on Linux?

Yes. Get the linux-x64.tar.gz package, unpack it, and open Lantern Engine, or run ./lantern.sh /path/to/CUSA03173 from a terminal. It runs natively on Wayland and X11.

DLSS Super Resolution works on Linux on RTX cards with NVIDIA's driver. DLSS Frame Generation is Windows only.

Graphics settings guide

Not sure what to pick? Start with Graphics › Best settings for this PC › Recommend… and change things from there.

Which upscaler should I use?

Graphics › Upscaler. The upscaler renders the game below your screen's resolution and rebuilds a sharp image, which gives you more FPS.

Your graphics cardBest choice
NVIDIA RTX (20 series and newer)DLSS
AMD RX 9000 seriesFSR 4
Other GPUs with the needed Vulkan featuresFSR 4.1.1 (experimental; the launcher says if your GPU can run it)
Any other cardFSR 3
Fast card, sharpest image without upscalingDLSS or FSR with the Native AA preset, or TAA
Quality preset: Native AA, Quality, Balanced, Performance…

Graphics › Quality preset sets how far below your screen the game renders.

  • Native AA: no upscaling, only anti-aliasing (with DLSS this is DLAA). Best image, lowest FPS.
  • Quality (x1.5) and Balanced (x1.7): a good mix for most PCs.
  • Performance (x2) and Ultra Performance (x3): for slower cards, or 4K screens.
Output resolution

Display › Output resolution is the size of the finished picture, usually your screen's. The upscaler renders below it and fills it in. On cards with 3–4 GB of video memory, 1080p is the safe choice.

Frame rate: 30, 60, 90, 120, 144 or Uncapped?

Display › Frame rate. 30, 60, 90, 120 and 144 are the game's own frame-rate patches. Pick the one your PC holds steadily that is not above your monitor's refresh rate.

  • Uncapped has no fixed rate, but it never goes above your monitor's refresh rate: on a 60 Hz monitor it stops at 60. To go past it anyway, add BB_CAP_TO_REFRESH=0 in Settings › Extra variables.
  • Uncapped can freeze at the end of a boss intro (Vicar Amelia's). 120 and 144 use another patch and don't.
Presentation mode (VSync)

On the Display page, Presentation mode:

  • Mailbox (default): low latency without tearing.
  • FIFO (VSync): classic vertical sync. On a 60 Hz monitor at 60 FPS it can be smoother than Mailbox, which drops a frame now and then.
  • FIFO Relaxed: VSync that tears instead of stuttering when a frame is late.
  • Immediate: lowest latency, may tear.
Frame generation and NVIDIA Reflex

Graphics › Frame generation adds generated frames between the game's own. It needs an upscaler (or TAA) on.

  • DLSS frame generation: NVIDIA RTX 40 and 50 series, with Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling on in Windows. RTX 50 can generate several frames, and dynamic frame generation picks how many for your refresh rate.
  • FSR 3.1 frame generation: any GPU. Not in menus or movies.
  • NVIDIA Reflex lowers input lag, and also keeps NVIDIA cards at their boost clocks.

Frame generation feels best when the game already runs at 50–60 FPS or more.

HDR

Display › HDR output. Windows HDR has to be on (Windows Settings › Display › Use HDR). Turning it on or off applies after restarting the game.

In-game menu (Insert or L3 + R3)

Press Insert on the keyboard, or L3 + R3 on a controller, to change graphics settings while you play: upscaler, sharpening, the FPS counter and more. Most changes apply at once.

Common questions

Shaders, co-op, online play, mods, keys and saves.

Why does the game stutter when I enter a new area?

The first time the game shows something new, the engine builds its shaders, which can cause a short hitch. It only happens once: shaders are saved and built at the start the next time.

  • After an engine update the first start takes longer: it rebuilds the saved shaders before the game starts, so you don't get them as stutters in play.
  • A small Building shaders note shows in the corner while it happens. A stutter with the note is shader building; one without it is loading.
  • You can share shaders: Settings › Shader catalog › Export catalog, and a friend with the same kind of graphics card adds it with Add catalog.
Should I clear the shader cache?

Usually not. A new engine version recompiles it by itself. Clear it (Settings › Shader cache › Clear) only if something draws wrong in every save, such as missing skin or hair. The next start compiles everything again and stutters for a while.

How do I share my shader catalog?

Your shaders go into the next release, so players with the same kind of graphics card stutter less the first time they visit an area.

  1. Play for a while first: the more areas you have visited, the more your catalog holds.
  2. In the launcher: Settings › Shader catalog › Export catalog, and save the .zip.
  3. Send the .zip as a DM to awakenkn on Discord, and say which graphics card you have (for example "RTX 4070" or "RX 7800 XT").

Catalogs from AMD and Intel cards help the most: Intel cards don't have one yet.

Are my saves backed up?

Yes. The launcher backs up your saves at each start, and every 10 minutes while you play when they changed. Find them in Play › Save backups.

How do I play with a friend (co-op)?
  1. Both players: in the game's Settings › Network, set the same Password Matching, and Matching Regions to Worldwide.
  2. The host rings a Beckoning Bell, the helper rings a Small Resonant Bell. Both can be bought in the Insight shop.
  3. Wait for the bells to resonate. It can take a while.
How do I play online (The Hunter's Dream)?
  1. In the launcher open the Online page. If you have no account yet, press Open website and sign up on The Hunter's Dream.
  2. Connect your Hunter's Dream account on the Online page.
  3. Start the game and choose Play Online. A welcome message appears when you are connected.

Server problems go to The Hunter's Dream's own Discord.

Does the Seamless Co-op mod work?

Not yet.

How do I install mods?

Unpack each mod into its own folder inside mods, so that it holds dvdroot_ps4:

mods\Mod name\dvdroot_ps4\chr\…

Then turn it on and set the load order in More › Mods. The lowest mod in the list wins when two mods change the same file. ZIP or 7z archives have to be unpacked first. Mods that replace eboot.bin, DLL injectors and script plugins don't work. Mods work on exFAT and FAT32 drives too.

How do I change keys?

In Controls, click an action and press the new key or mouse button.

The + next to an action adds another key or a combination like Shift+E or Ctrl+Mouse4, up to five per action. A combination wins over its plain key.

Mouse sensitivity goes down to -10 for high-DPI mice; each step below 0 is 20% slower. Mouse side buttons are bound in the Elden Ring style profile.

The Debug Menu won't start

The debug menu needs the font files DbgFont14h.ccm and DbgFont14h.tpf in dvdroot_ps4\font, either in your game folder or in an enabled mod (More › Mods). The fonts are on Nexus Mods.

They have to be the packed files. A DbgFont14h-tpf-dcx folder (unpacked with WitchyBND) does not work: repack it. Don't copy them into an out\mod-game-… folder. The engine makes that folder new at every start.

Trophies

The Trophies page lists them with their pictures. You can use your own trophy sound in WAV, MP3 or M4A, and set its volume.

Fixing problems

First steps for almost anything: update the launcher and engine, update your GPU driver, and try once with mods off (or Use safe mode after a crash).

The game opens, shows a black screen and closes
  • Check that the engine is current: the Play page shows Engine update available when it isn't.
  • Update the graphics driver.
  • An antivirus can block the engine's files. Allow the Lantern Engine folder in it.
  • Don't install into Program Files or a OneDrive folder.
  • If it started after a beta update, check the Beta guide: it may be a known beta problem, and you can go back to the version before.
"The graphics driver stopped responding" (0x17)

The GPU hung or lost the game's memory.

  1. Update to the newest version and update the NVIDIA or AMD driver.
  2. If it keeps happening, try once with BB_GUEST_IN_PLACE=0 in Settings › Extra variables, and say in your report whether it helped.
"Out of video memory", or crashes on a 3–4 GB graphics card
  • Lower Display › Output resolution to 1080p or less, and use a lower Quality preset (Performance).
  • Use fewer texture or model mods: HD texture packs need much more video memory.
  • Close browsers and recorders while playing.
  • Keep More › Engine › Use more VRAM for textures off on cards with 4–6 GB.
"Windows could not give the game its memory" at the start

The game needs about 6 GB of RAM plus page file (about 10 GB above 1080p).

  • A game left running in the background holds it: end bb-probe.exe in Task Manager (Details), or restart the PC.
  • Set the page file to System managed size: Windows Settings › System › About › Advanced system settings › Performance Settings › Advanced › Virtual memory › Change.
  • Or choose a lower output resolution or upscaler preset.
The game crashes while loading (missing or damaged game files)

If the crash report mentions missing files or FileTransferTask, the game folder is incomplete or damaged. Turn mods off first. If it still crashes, dump or extract the game again (base game plus the 1.09 update), and keep it out of OneDrive.

Flickering, stretched or missing textures
  • Update to the newest version. 0.9.37 fixes shaders reading the wrong buffers, which caused stretched geometry and flicker, especially on older AMD cards (RX 400/500) and on NVIDIA.
  • If it happens in every save (missing skin or hair), clear the shader cache once: Settings › Shader cache › Clear.
  • Try with mods off, and with frame generation off.
  • Still there? Post a short video and your log.
Small stutters at 60 FPS on a 60 Hz monitor

Set Presentation mode to FIFO (VSync) on the Display page. At 60 FPS on a 60 Hz screen, Mailbox can drop a frame every so often, and that feels like a micro-stutter. Stutters the first time you see an area are shader building, see Why does the game stutter.

The game is stuck at 30 FPS

The game runs slower while its window is in the background, to save power. Click into the game window. FPS staying low after you switch back, or after lantern travel, is fixed in 0.9.37.

"Cannot create save file"

The saves folder can't be written, and the launcher now says why. Move Lantern Engine out of OneDrive and other synced folders, and out of Program Files. If Windows' Controlled folder access is on, allow the Lantern Engine programs in it.

Mods don't load ("cannot link … dvdroot_ps4")

This happened on exFAT and FAT32 drives before 0.9.37, which reads mod files in place there: update. Also check that each mod's folder holds dvdroot_ps4 (see How do I install mods?). The start's log says how many game files each mod replaced.

Linux: the window closes at the start on Wayland

If the log says Wayland display connection closed by server, the compositor ended the connection, a driver and compositor mismatch (NVIDIA 615 drivers on KDE Plasma did this; 580 runs clean). Try another driver version, or start with SDL_VIDEODRIVER=x11 ./lantern.sh to run through XWayland.

Known issues

Reported and not fixed yet. Lantern Engine is moving to its own native engine, so expect bugs even in stable releases. A log or a video of any of these helps.

  • Some lights, smoke and lamp glow missing, and blocky lighting, on some PCs.
  • Green or white flashes at doors in the Research Hall, and white or blue screens in the Forbidden Woods.
  • FPS dropping after long sessions on some PCs.
  • 4K with DLSS or FSR above Performance: light dotted outlines around tree branches.
  • Steam Deck: a crash in Game Mode after the intro (Desktop Mode works).
  • Falling snow missing in Cainhurst.
  • Crashes on graphics cards with 3 GB of video memory.

What the beta is

Test builds that come before a stable release.

Should I use the beta?

Betas are test builds that come before a stable release. They have the newest fixes and features, and sometimes new bugs. Use one if a problem you have is fixed in it, or if you want to help test. Stay on stable if you just want to play.

Your settings, saves, mods and logs are kept when you switch between beta and stable.

Join or leave the beta

One setting in the launcher, and you can go back any time.

Get the beta
  1. In the launcher: Settings › Updates.
  2. Set Update channel to Beta.
  3. Press Check now and install the update.

On Linux, the launcher updates itself from 0.9.37 beta 7 on. Before that, get the Linux package from Releases and unpack it over your folder.

Go back to stable, or to the version before

Set Update channel back to Stable to get stable updates again. To install a particular version right away (for example the beta before, when a new one breaks your game): Settings › Updates › Version, pick it, and press Install.

No beta right now

The newest version is the stable 0.9.37; on the beta channel the launcher installs it too.

Reporting beta problems

Say which beta, and whether the one before had it.

What helps
  • The crash report or log (see Before you post). Both start with your version, so we know which beta it was.
  • Whether it also happened on the version before. If not, say so: it means the new beta caused it.
  • Post in #issues on the Discord.
If a beta breaks your game, go back to the version before (Join or leave the beta) and keep playing; your saves are kept.
